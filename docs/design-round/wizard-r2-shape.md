# Start a game, the gallery: shape brief (Round 2)

- **Job and audience:** Operate. Whoever starts games for a couch, on a phone around an episode, often in passing (Andrew: one tap),
  choosing for people who aren't there (Joanna must never fall behind), sometimes building something exact (Conor: every piece). The
  page spec ([wizard-r2-spec.md](wizard-r2-spec.md)) and the first-view requirements ([wizard-r2-first-view.md](wizard-r2-first-view.md),
  R1 to R5) are the brief's content; this sets how it looks and moves.
- **Selected direction:** inside the committed world, Lower Third on Night (DESIGN.md). The gallery is a broadcast's programme guide
  of games: dark squares on the sheet's panel, each a solid pictogram (the user's style, 2026-11-17), the known name in the condensed
  caps, the plain line in body text, a length mark quiet when it's the season and a bone badge when it isn't. One grammar across the
  screens: squares choose, rows open, chips pick one, bone is chosen, dashed can't be had and says why, yellow is Start and nothing
  else. A base's screen leads with its sentence, what everyone does (a three-bar weekly mark and Try it), the season strip under its
  stages, and the lock line; its rows below are the expert workspace. A change opens its squares with the chosen one's controls
  directly under it.
- **Scope:** TEMP-UI behind `?start=gallery` on the real app (the Start a game sheet), saving nothing; every screen of the spec at 390
  on the real Survivor 51, Below Deck 13 and The Challenge 42. The practice copy behind the preview, the sport's gallery and the
  nano-banana icons are the build's (vector stand-ins drawn here: the Gemini key's billing was paused on 2026-11-17). Untouched:
  today's wizard, which stays the default; the engine's rules.
- **States:** a base that plays, coming, or needs setup; a draft fitted to who's left; a length or a choice the engine refuses,
  dashed with its reason; someone behind (named closed only when it costs them); our AI's wait, its result, away and out of asks;
  an aired season (one cell, nothing to choose); a story show (its own nouns, no Survivor examples).
- **Interaction:** the sheet's own levels (gallery, base, change, questions, lock, preview, ask, describe, community, done) with a
  named back; Done and Start docked; a changed row marked with Undo; chips in one sideways row; motion only what the sheet already
  does (its rise) and the Changed tag sliding in; 44 px targets; 16 px in the field.
