# How a couch plays sports: the shape brief (Impeccable, step 3)

On [the spec](sports-couch-play-spec.md) and [the first-view requirements](sports-couch-play-first-view.md). Mode: Operate. Lower
Third on Night, round 1's parts (the shared game row, the mark, the face). Idea mocks; nothing built.

- **Real content:** NFL Week 9, 2026, as ESPN listed it. The couch's records through Week 8 (Giants 4–2–1, Patriots 5–3, Cowboys
  3–5, Eagles 2–6, Jets 2–6) and their form; Giants at Eagles on Sun Nov 8 (the Giants won 19–11); the couch's rivalry calendar.
  The couch: you (Patriots, Yankees), Andrew (Giants), Riley (Eagles), Joanna (Cowboys), Sam (Jets). **Made up and marked:** every
  pick, call, side, record of calls, chat line and stake.
- **Three moments of one week:** Wed Nov 4 (the build-up), Sun Nov 8 at 4:15 PM (the rivalry's result), Tue Nov 10 (Andrew's next
  call, Thursday's Commanders at Giants).
- **New parts:** the fan's face on the side line (after the team's name); the couch's table row (rank, mark, team, faces, record on
  line one; form ticks and this week's game on line two); the rivalry card (two people over their teams and records, a sides bar
  with faces, Pick a side as two outlined buttons with marks, the duel's line); the challenge sheet; a fan card (whose team, calls
  record, season call); Back and Fade.
- **Rivalry mark:** the lucide swords icon, small, muted, never a slab. Form ticks: teal W, outlined L, a half-filled T.
- **Anti-goals:** no VS graphics, no slogans, no new colours (team colours on marks and the plate's end only; people colours on
  faces), one yellow per view, no countdowns.
