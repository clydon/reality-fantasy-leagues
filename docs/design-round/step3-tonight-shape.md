# Tonight: the visual brief (step 3)

- **Job and mode.** Operate. Someone opens a show for "is anything for me, where's everyone, how did last week go"; Joanna reads
  the top, Andrew taps the one bright thing, Conor opens a game's numbers. Structure: [the page spec](step3-tonight-spec.md);
  first view: [R1–R5](step3-tonight-first-view.md).
- **Direction: the couch stands on the show.** The committed world, Lower Third on Night (DESIGN.md). The tall show bar keeps
  the art and the name (smaller: 40 px, two lines at most), and its foot becomes a broadcast ruler: the season's episodes as
  ticks, the aired run solid, the next airing dashed, and the couch's faces (28 px) standing on their marks, you ringed in bone.
  One line of words above it ("Through Ep 4 · Ep 5 Sun 8:00 PM") and, when there is one, the one watching action as a plate
  button under it. This is the page's focal moment and its one motion: on entry the faces slide from the start of the ruler to
  their marks (320 ms, ease-out, none under reduced motion).
- **The strip is the alert strip** (DESIGN.md "The caption plate as the app's to-do list"): a chyron tag naming the episode, a
  white plate with the move in words and its lock, a chevron. Due: the tag is chyron, the screen's one yellow. All set and locked:
  the same shape on a night panel, a bone tick, no yellow. It sits in the same place under the bar on every tab but Chat.
- **Below the fold, hairline sections, no cards.** Last time: the out plate (the show's own graphic, kept), the couch's GIFs as a
  row of tiles named on their tag-cut plates (yours first, or a dashed "Yours" tile), then two doors side by side, Discuss Ep N
  and Ep N recap. Your games: one mini per game as rows between hairlines, the game's name in its ink, the matchup or standing in
  condensed numerals, the last result in a line; a Lineup toggle opens the ledger in place (each starter's face, name, a bar on one
  points scale shared across your starters, the value; negatives as a short outline left of zero, as the step 2c compare draws
  them). Next episode: rows. Stats: as is.
- **Colour.** Yellow only for what's due (the strip, or the reveal when nothing is due). Teal only for a win in a mini. Live red
  only for on air and Out. Faces wear people colours (no gold, no teal).
- **States and ranges.** 1 to 8 people on the ruler (two at one mark overlap by 10 px; more than three at one mark show three and
  "+n"); seasons of 6 to 26 episodes on one ruler (ticks thin to every fifth label past 14); no art (the show's colour); solo (you
  alone, no ruler words about a couch); a failed read (Try again).
- **Untouched.** The game page, the reveal, the Your moves sheet's insides, the where sheet, the stats card, Episodes and the
  other tabs' bodies.
