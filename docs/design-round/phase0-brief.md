# Phase 0 brief: the whole app's first views, in grey (2026-11-14)

## Job and audience
Every surface of Game Your Show, read by the same people at different distances: Joanna back after a week (scans the top for
"anything for me?"), Andrew between other things (taps the one bright thing), Conor deep in the numbers. Mode: Operate (app UI),
except the reveal (Experience) and the welcome page (Persuade). Phone first, 390 × 844.

## Outcome and proof
A first view per layer that passes [R1–R5](phase0-first-views.md): four chunks at most, an anchor of the show or its people,
one form per shared part, the way in's prediction met, a one-tap instinct path with the sweat's comparison beside it. Real content
only: the zt Survivor 51 couch (alex, riley, jordan, sam; Episode 6 Wednesday at 8 PM; results through 5), the bench's NFL week, a
talk show and a streaming drop.

## Direction: the identity question (the user's to decide)
The incumbent world is established: "Lower Third" (DESIGN.md), the design system since 2026-10-10 and the new app built on it
since 2026-10-26; the logo's chyron wedge is the same idea, so the brand commitments lean on it. The census separates what the world does well from what its use does badly:
- **The world earns its keep** where the show's art, a face or a white plate carries one moment: the hub's Up next with Survivor's
  art, the red OUT plate, the Your moves face grid, the reveal's beats, the welcome page. Those are the app's 50 ms "TV night".
- **The busy comes from how much of the world is spent at once**: a white plate or a yellow slab on nearly every card (the move,
  the reveal, Waivers, Pick, Set, Lineup), a two-line display title plus its ghost copy in every header, 11 type sizes in the median
  first view against the system's own scale, yellow used for a due move, Save, Start it, Claim and decoration alike. None of these
  is the world; each is the world used without a budget.
- **Keeping the world and reopening density** means: a plate is a moment, not a card; one yellow per view, kept for what's due for
  you; display type only for the one anchor; the type scale held to about five sizes per view. **Reopening the look** means a new
  world through new-work, keeping the name, the logo and the brand commitments.

## Scope and boundaries
Grey boxes only: structure, real labels and the eye's order, numbered. No colour, art or type choices beyond weight for hierarchy.
One first view per layer for competition shows, a sports check where the part changes (the game page, Players), and one story
frame. Untouched: the five-tab structure (hub → show → Tonight / Episodes / Games / people / Chat), the all-games reveal, own pace
by default. Anti-goals: a clinical dashboard; a theatrical page that buries the episode; any new overview section.

## States and ranges
Caught up with a move due; two behind with results waiting; watching only with friends at different paces; head to head this
week; a sports week (a lineup of nine, kickoffs Thursday to Monday); a pool of 18 faces against a pool of 300 athletes; a story
with no one leaving.

## Interaction and layout intent
Each grey box marks the fold. Below it, the depth: rich and dense, reached by tapping the thing on screen and returning to the
same place. One quick-move surface (the strip opens the sheet). One progress control per show. One person sheet.

Listed with recommendations in the screenshot story: the identity question first.
