## The one batch

- The night's number carries its unit ("−3 pts"), and "so far" while results are early; a place that held shows no mark (the dash
  read as a minus); a drop is at full strength, not grey.
- Someone leaving reads in live red ("Went home", "Lost 1": DESIGN.md's Red Is For Leaving); a pool's pick is named under its face.
- A quick game takes the season cards' grammar: your place left, who won in words beside it ("shared with alex and sam", never
  "Tied" beside "T1"), your points right, "Everyone's night" to open; its name without the episode it was named for.
- The join sheet names a one-episode game by its own name ("Join Who survives?", "It plays Episode 7 only"), never its base.
- The best night by the episode sheet's rule (the most points, everyone tied named).
- Each fact once: your own row in everyone's night drops its faces (your night is right above it); the couch's face stack on every
  card goes; a team game is measured by its points (who's still in is its faces); bingo's title drops the episode the section names.
- Cut text: the ledger's bench says "Bench" (its faint row says the rest); the Join row says "2 in · pick before you watch".
- Faces in everyone's rows a size up (26 px), badges clear of them; the fold's chips name their games and wrap.
- Trivia reads "2/3", the best in teal; a round not played says "Play this week's round"; game names in the foreground.

## Left as they are

- The ledger's bars (a negative as an outline, the bench's bars faint): data round 3's approved component; PFD read the outline as an
  empty field and the bench's longest bar as misleading. A choice for the user.
- The strip's "Pick who survives" (the strip's words, not this section's); the bingo row's "Ready … Ready" (the next episode's row).
- Survival Pool's game colour is teal, the colour of good news (the game palette predates the people palette's rule).
- The episode sheet's header and its rows' "that episode" places (a kept level of the drill).
- Font sizes off DESIGN.md's scale (14 px, 17 px, 12 px): the new design's existing sizes, used across Tonight.
- Joanna's "did anything happen to me?" is answered about a screen down: Tonight's order is settled (Product Principle 13). A line of
  results in the top strip would answer it at once and change what the strip is for: a choice for the user.
