# Start a game, the bold pass: shape (Impeccable)

Mode: **Operate** (start a game), with the lead tiles allowed a little of Persuade's appetite (they sell a kind of game). World:
the committed one, Lower Third on Night (DESIGN.md). Inputs: [wizard-r2b-spec.md](wizard-r2b-spec.md),
[wizard-r2b-first-view.md](wizard-r2b-first-view.md) R1 to R5.

## Where the boldness goes

One place per screen, everything around it quiet:
- **The gallery:** each category's lead tile, made of the season's own faces, questions and moments. Squares stay calm.
- **A base's screen:** the title card, the game's name in title-card caps over the show's greyscale art, as the started game's
  own page will look.
- Nothing else gets louder. Yellow stays on Start; plates stay for the ready game and moments that matter.

## The parts

- **Lead tile:** full width, the night panel, 4 px corners, at least 136 px tall, the whole tile one button. Top: the base's name in
  26 px condensed caps with its length mark (the squares' own corner). Middle: the picture (below). Bottom: the plain line, 15 px
  in plain ink. No plate (plates stay rare), no shadow.
- **The pictures:** faces as cast tiles without their plate (34 px photos, 3 px corners, the people still in as of the person's
  mark, a "+4" chip past what fits); questions as up to three hairline rows; a card as a 3 × 3 grid of the show's moments in 12 px
  condensed caps; the couch as colour dots and names paired with "vs"; a bet as one line in tabular numbers; the season as the
  strip's bands. A sport's: a matchup line with each team's colour as a tag, a stat line, a corner of the squares grid.
- **Squares:** the name a step up (21 px condensed caps), the line 15 px in plain ink, two lines at most.
- **Title card:** the show's art as a texture (greyscale, under a wash to the panel), the name at 52 px (44 px past 12 characters),
  "Also called" in 13 px, the sentence at 16 px, then what everyone does with Try it.
- **Make it…:** three 64 px rows, an icon tile, the goal in 16 px semibold, its next step in 14 px ("Next: head to head each
  episode"), and a plus where a row would have a chevron (it acts here, it doesn't open anything). Dashed with why when nothing's
  left. A tap: a toast with Undo, and the rule it changed marked.
- **The rules:** each row's label in 12 px caps, its value at 16 px semibold, the rest of its line in 13 px; Changed and Undo as in
  Round 2. All rows shown.
- **Find a change:** the gallery's own field, 16 px, "Find a change: captain, bets, lives"; results as the change squares, two
  across.
- **Sports:** the team's colour as a tag (the ready plate's angled tag may take it, DESIGN.md's caption plate rule), never a fill.

## Motion and states

No new motion: the sheet's own slide and the toast. States: a lead tile whose game can't play here falls to the category's first
game that does; a show with no faces (nobody cast yet) draws its lead tiles from moments and episodes; no art means the plain
panel.
