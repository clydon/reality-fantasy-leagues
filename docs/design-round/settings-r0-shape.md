# Settings, Round 0: the grey boxes' brief (Impeccable `shape`)

> **Status (2026-11-20): Round 0, step 3 of the design process.** Impeccable's shape, on Intent's structure
> ([settings-r0-spec.md](settings-r0-spec.md)) and PFD's first views ([settings-r0-first-view.md](settings-r0-first-view.md)).
> The user confirms at the screenshot story, as DESIGN-PROCESS.md has it, not mid-round. Assumptions are marked.

1. **Job and audience.** These are Operate surfaces. People arrive with a reason (a nuisance, a fear, a fix) and leave when it's
   done. Runners arrive with a task. Round 0's own job is narrower: let the user choose a structure. So the frames compare three
   shapes on the same four situations, with real words.
2. **Outcome and proof.** The user can see, frame by frame:
   - which shape puts each reason's fix nearest;
   - which reads as "my settings" in five seconds;
   - which keeps a runner's density off a player's screen.

   The proof is the same person and the same data in every frame.
3. **Direction.**
   - **The visual authority is DESIGN.md's "Lower Third".** The night ground; the subject's identity on top (a caption plate for
     the show and the game, your face and name for You); hairline rows; values in dusk-muted. Chyron yellow appears only on the one
     real problem (no phone gets your notifications).
   - **The thesis:** a home per subject, opened from the subject (Shape 1), set against one scoped Settings (Shape 2) and a
     questions-first You (Shape 3).
   - **Implementation:** none. These are mock pages, rendered at 390 for the story.
4. **Scope.** 14 frames:
   - **Shape 1 (4):** You · This show (the ⋯ sheet) · This couch · Manage this game.
   - **Shape 2 (5):** Settings opened from the hub (You), from the show, from the couch and from the game, plus the gear that opens
     it.
   - **Shape 3 (5):** the questions; "Get fewer notifications" opened; plus the three subject frames it shares with Shape 1, marked
     as shared.

   They're low fidelity: real labels and values, real hierarchy, simple glyphs, no final styling.
   - **Anti-goals:** a polished look that reads as approved; invented data; cheeky names.
5. **States and ranges.** One person throughout, **alex**, from new-fixture.js:
   - owns the couch "ZT Survivor 51", and is also on "ZT Extra" with casey;
   - plays and runs three games on ZT Survivor's Season 51 (Outlast, Weekly Lineup, Survival Pool);
   - is through Episode 5, with Episode 6 on Wednesday at 8 PM;
   - has notifications off on this phone, so You shows its one yellow plate;
   - has two exceptions set elsewhere (*assumed, to show the map*): ZT Extra's chat set to Never, and ZT Traitors hidden from the
     couch.

   Manage this game is Weekly Lineup: draft 5, start 3, the lock at airtime.
6. **Interaction and layout.**
   - Each group is one collapsed row, value first, with a chevron. Nothing is open in a first view.
   - Things to do, settings and leaving never interleave. Leaving is last, in live red.
   - Shape 2 has a scope bar under its header: You · Show · Couch · Game, the current one marked.
   - Shape 3's questions are rows written in the person's words.
   - The fold line at 844 is drawn on each frame's full page.
7. **Constraints and open decisions.**
   - 390 × 844, dark, Archivo (condensed caps for titles), 44 px targets.
   - The names are candidates: Impeccable's clarify settles them in this round, and the user picks.
   - *Assumed:* the You door stays the hub's picture, and the couch's door is its name (the spec's open questions 1 and 2).
