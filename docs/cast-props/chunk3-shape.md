# Cast properties, chunk 3: the cast tab and the person sheet (Impeccable shape, 2026-11-16)

A small addition to the design round's step 5 (Players / Cast, approved direction), so it goes through Impeccable alone
(docs/DESIGN-PROCESS.md, "When not to use them"), phone first at 390. The plan's chunk 3 and its "Bios" section are the user's
decisions (2026-11-15); what's below marked **assumed** wasn't asked, and is carried to the screenshot story as an open choice.

## Job and audience

- Watchers and players on the show's Cast tab, and anyone tapping a face in the draft room or a game: "who is this?" Mode:
  Operate (browse, then drill). Most acute before the premiere, when a draft runs and there are no numbers yet: today the wall's
  faces open nothing until the first results (the "bare" wall), and the draft room's sheet says only "Toka · Still in".

## Outcome and proof

- A glance at the wall tells each person's place in the show's own terms (Below Deck: department as the grouping, the job
  under each name); a tap tells who they are as the show bills them ("Brisbane · 25 · Nurse", and its one line).
- Done when a researched Below Deck season reads by department with jobs (Below Deck 13, our bot's answer reviewed on the desk),
  and a castaway's sheet in a pre-premiere draft says who they are.

## Direction (inside Lower Third; no new visual world)

- **The wall's groups:** tribes when the show has them (unchanged). With none, the first property that has values, in the
  rulebook's order of them (Bridge, Interior, Deck, Galley), each a condensed-caps heading like a tribe's but with no colour
  square: colour stays a tribe's or team's alone (DESIGN.md). Anyone with no value stated sits in the leftover group, as today.
- **The line under a name:** the main property's value (a crew's job), else a sport's position (unchanged), else nothing;
  "· Ep 4" when out, as today. One line, truncated.
- **Filter chips:** only for a listed property the wall doesn't already group by (The Challenge: Veteran · Rookie under its
  teams), one quiet row above the wall: All, then each value with how many. Single choice; the wall keeps its groups, showing
  only that value's people. Below Deck gets none (department is the grouping, job is free text). **Assumed:** one row per such
  property, none for free-text properties.
- **The person sheet's head:** the group and the person's properties on one line ("Interior · Chief Stew"), then the bio line
  ("Brisbane · 25 · Nurse"), then the billing's own line in the body face, at most three lines. Nothing for what's not stated:
  no "Unknown". The source stays on the desk. **Assumed:** no "From Bravo" credit on the sheet.
- **Before the first results:** the wall's faces open the person sheet (who they are; their numbers fill in as they watch),
  instead of opening nothing. The draft room and game sheets get the same head, since they render the same person body.

## Scope and boundaries

- Untouched: The numbers, the lens, compare, the weekly graph, tribe colours, the sports list, secret roles (a secret role is
  never a property and never shows early).
- Anti-goals: no new overview section on the Cast tab; no badges or tags per face; no colour for property values.

## States and ranges

- No properties and no bio (Survivor before research): exactly today's tab. A bio with only a hometown (most of Bravo's).
  A long hometown ("Bournemouth, South Coast England") wraps; a long billing line clamps at three lines. 30 people in four
  teams with a status each (The Challenge). Someone out: their sheet still says who they are.
