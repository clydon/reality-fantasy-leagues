Waivers, two ways

Survivor 51's Weekly Lineup, the week after Episode 4: Jenna's out, a waiver window is open, claims are due the day before Episode 5. Left is the simple version, my best attempt at casual-first: the best free agent is already lined up, so doing nothing is a fine answer. Right is the v2 port: the live app's waivers (PLAN-waivers-v2, the picked design) moved into the new design. Both share the results (a reveal, then an entry in Episodes) and How it works. Every phone is the real mock: tap anything.

1 · The page, claims open

Pushed in from the moves dock or Tonight's "Before Wednesday" list. One screen answers "what do I do, and by when".

Simple Recommended

  • Zero taps needed. Sharonda (most points of anyone free) is lined up for Jenna's spot; it's your claim unless you change it.
  • Backups are automatic: if someone ahead takes her, you get the best one left. Choosing your own is a switch in the picker.
  • Picking order is a picture: four faces, you're 3rd, lowest score first. No "rolling" to explain.
  • The swap is one optional row.

v2 port

  • The plate: Replace Jenna · Swap 1, due Tue, you're 3rd in line.
  • Your team with Swap on everyone still in, Replace on Jenna.
  • Claims grouped by spot, ranked, with a grip to reorder or remove (shown filled in).
  • Nothing happens unless you claim (auto-fill is Casual-only).

2 · Picking someone

Simple opens a sheet over the page; v2 pushes a picker page with Research's controls.

Simple

  • A ranked list with a bar: This season or Last 3 episodes. One tap, then "Take Devin".
  • "Choose my own backups" turns taps into 1, 2, 3.

v2 port

  • "Tap your first choice, then any backups." Tap order is the rank.
  • Season · Last 3 · Last ep, and Ranked by any stat from the research (full names, from the explorer).
  • A confirm bar: For Jenna · backup Devin · Save.

3 · Swapping someone still in

Simple

  • Two short steps: who goes (their points go with them), then who comes in.
  • One swap, shown as the same out → in card as the replacement.

v2 port

  • Swap on any team row opens the same picker; swap backups can drop different people ("Linnea for Mike, only if Cristian's taken").

4 · A shared league

Nobody can lose a free agent to anyone, so both drop ranking, backups and the line.

Simple

  • "Nobody else can take Sharonda." No picking order.

v2 port

  • "You get who you pick." Pick one; the row reads "Sharonda for Jenna · joins your team Tue".

5 · After the run

The page once claims have run. The reveal and the Episodes entry (section 8) are the same for both.

Simple

  • The out → in card, now green: Devin's on your team, and why not Sharonda. Then everyone's moves as sentences.

v2 port

  • A plate and one line of why, then Free agency and Past waivers, as in the live app.

6 · Commissioner: waivers settings

Simple

  • One choice: the feel. Picking one applies it; no Apply step.
  • The three settings fold into Fine-tune.

v2 port

  • 1A: the Suggested card with Casual · Balanced · Competitive and Apply, then every setting on the page.

7 · Commissioner: open a waiver window

Simple

  • Three presets: Replacements · + a swap · Rebuild. "Something else" opens the controls.

v2 port

  • 3B: a Replace toggle and a swaps counter (None · 1 · 2 … · All). Both preview the no-names line players get.

In Episodes

  • "Waivers · after Episode 4" between 5 and 4; its page lists every move in picking order.

How it works

  • Three answers for this league, then When moves happen: Replace and Swap lanes over the episodes.
