The guide in a thin week

With one or two shows, a seven-column guide is mostly "Nothing on". All three keep the guide: day columns, Tonight first, the now line. They differ in what an empty day becomes. Busier couches keep today's rolling guide unchanged. Each phone is the working mock.

1Slim days + picksRecommended

The two soonest empty days each get one pick: a narrower, faded card for a show airing that day, ranked by how like your shows it is ("Like Survivor"), with "3 more on Fri ›" for that day's full listings (filters, search). Every other empty day is a slim strip. "Hide picks" makes them all slim.

GoodDiscovery by default, two picks a week at most however big TV gets, and your own shows are never crowded out.
Watch outPicks need a good "like your shows" ranking to feel personal.

2Slim days

With two or fewer shows, empty days shrink to strips with the day written down them; days with shows widen. The whole week fits on one screen and the guide stops rolling.

GoodCalm and honest: "nothing until Wednesday" at a glance.
Watch outNo discovery: a one-show couch may never try anything else.

3On TV fill-ins

With two or fewer shows, every empty day keeps a full column holding one faded On TV show with a +.

GoodDiscovery, full-size.
Watch outPicks look as big as your own shows; no way into the rest of the day's listings.
