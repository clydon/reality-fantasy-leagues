The guide in a thin week

With one or two shows, a seven-column guide is mostly "Nothing on". Both options keep the guide: day columns, Tonight first, the now line. They differ in what an empty day becomes. Busier couches keep today's rolling guide unchanged. Each phone is the working mock.

1Slim daysRecommended

An empty day shrinks to a strip with the day written down it; days with your shows widen. The whole week fits on one screen, so you see "nothing until Wednesday" at a glance, and the guide stops rolling.

GoodHonest and calm. Full-size cards. Grows naturally into the rolling guide as shows are added.
Watch outDoesn't help you find more to watch.

2On TV fill-ins

Every day keeps its column. An empty day shows one faded On TV show that airs that day but nobody on the couch watches, with a + to add it.

GoodEmpty days become discovery: great for a brand-new couch.
Watch outShows the couch doesn't watch, in "This week on the couch". Needs real TV listings data.
