Streaming shows in the guide

Shows dropped all at once (Harry Potter, Severance) or long finished (The Wire) have no airtime, so they can't sit in the TV guide like weekly shows. Who's watching them still matters: it's how riley's Severance becomes your Severance. All three options keep two things: a drop day is a real event in the guide ("All 8 drop · Fri 9:00 PM"), and a streaming show's page shows everyone's progress. The difference is where self-paced watching shows up on the hub. Each phone below is the working mock; scroll and tap inside it.
